about beardo:

beardo is a male smug type bear villager who was originally introduced in New Leaf and then became a permanent installation for the rest of the series (so far). the most current release in the Animal Crossing series that has Beardo in it is Animal Crossing: New Horizons.

Beardo's name is a play on his species and his facial hair. His Japanese name, Baird, sounds similar to both words [beard and bear] and relates to his mustache; the Japanese language does not make a distinction between the mustache and beard, which may explain his lack of a beard, contradicting his English namesake.
source

my love for him:

Beardo was actually the first ever villager I got in my campsite when I started New Horizons. I got ACNH about 3 months after release, and since it was 2020, Animal Crossing was one of the only things I did. I didn't have any prior experience with the game besides Pocket Camp, and being able to create my own island was a very special experience for
me during quarantine. At first, Beardo and I were just simple accquaintances,
but it quickly became a really good friendship. Which is weird since I'm
saying this about a fictional bear, but I believe there's something special
about the bond you make with villagers in Animal Crossing.

He's such a silly little guy. Fun fact, bears are also my favorite animal!
I thought it was so cute the way he would put on his little glasses and sit
around to read his little books. He would always ask me if I had had a
good day, and never really cared if I sat around his house all the time.
Also, just look?? At his face??? Is it not adorable??? And his little
clothes??? His sideburns wrap all the way around his head. He's so
cute. His catchphrase is literally "whiskers." Don't even talk to me
he makes me emotional. I love my fictional bear best friend!!!!
